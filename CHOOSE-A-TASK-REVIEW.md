# Choose a task or make a choice: six variants

Six standalone square transparent PNGs across all three age strands, with male and female versions for ages 5, 10 and teens. Each character points to a book picture card, selecting between two task options represented by a book and pencil. All are **drafts pending owner review**.

## Style and validation

Generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool using the approved homework illustration as a style reference. Requested #202020 contours, #1769AA blue and #F28C28 orange, opaque white skin, shoes and card faces, and neutral gray table and trousers. Each is 1254 × 1254 RGBA with genuine background transparency. Full character, table and two options are inside the frame; no embedded text. Some tonal variation and minor edge-color artifacts remain; exact hex colors are not guaranteed. Proportions vary by age.

Full prompts, descriptions, source information and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `catalog/visuals.json`. Category: Learning and tasks.
