# Put dirty clothes in hamper: six variants

Six standalone square transparent PNGs across all three age strands, with male and female versions for ages 5, 10 and teens. Each fully dressed character lowers an orange shirt into a blue laundry hamper; a white sock at the rim reinforces the laundry context. All are **drafts pending owner review**.

## Style and validation

Generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool using the approved homework illustration as a style reference. Requested #202020 contours, #1769AA blue and #F28C28 orange, opaque white skin, shoes and sock, and neutral gray trousers. Each is 1254 × 1254 RGBA with genuine surrounding background transparency. Full character and hamper are inside the frame; no embedded text. Some tonal variation remains; exact hex colors are not guaranteed. Proportions vary by age.

Full prompts, descriptions, source information and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `catalog/visuals.json`. Category: Daily routines.
