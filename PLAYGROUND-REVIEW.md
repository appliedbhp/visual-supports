## Style and validation

Generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool using the approved homework illustration as a style reference. Requested #202020 contours, #1769AA blue and #F28C28 orange, opaque white skin and neutral gray trousers. Each is 1254 × 1254 RGBA with genuine background transparency. Full child and slide are contained in the frame; no embedded text. Some subtle tonal variation remains; exact hex colors are not guaranteed. Older children have longer limbs and smaller relative heads.

Full prompts, descriptions, source information and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `catalog/visuals.json`. Category: Recreation.
